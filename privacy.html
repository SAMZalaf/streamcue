Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · version 1.0.0 · net.zalaf.streamcue

Short version: no account, no sign-up, no name or contacts. Your recordings, favourites and settings stay on your device. The only outbound traffic is an ad request to Adsterra and, if you use a Pro code, a hash-only activation check.

1. Who we are

StreamCue Studio is an Android soundboard developed by Mohamad Zalaf. Contact: MohamadZalaf@outlook.com.

2. What we do NOT collect

No name, email, phone, birth date, photos, contacts, messages, precise location or call logs. Your recorded audio never leaves your device. We do not sell data.

3. Stored on your device only

Settings, favourites, your own recordings (WAV/Blob), local counters and your Pro licence state live in the WebView localStorage. Uninstalling the app or clearing the cache removes them.

4. Microphone

The microphone is requested only when you tap record in the Voice tab, never at launch and never in the background. Audio is processed on-device (pitch, filters, WAV export) and is not uploaded. Denying the permission leaves every other feature working.

5. Advertising (Adsterra)

Ads are served by Adsterra, an independent third party that may automatically process your IP address, device and OS type, system language, an advertising/device identifier and connection type — for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We never see or store that data. See Adsterra's policy. Rewarded ads and offer opens happen only when you tap them; no ad window opens on its own.

6. Sound downloads

Some clips are fetched on demand from public audio hosts (e.g. myinstants.com). Your IP address is visible to that host as with any download; their policy applies. Downloaded files are cached on your device.

7. Pro activation

Entering a code sends only the SHA-256 hash of the code to our verification registry — no name, no email, no device identifier. The hash cannot be reversed into a code. The server stores the result (active/revoked) and may keep ordinary IP logs for security and abuse prevention. Offline means no activation and no transmission.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data about them.

9. Your rights (incl. GDPR)

Access and deletion are immediate: your data is on your device, so clearing it or uninstalling removes it; we hold no copy. You may object to personalised ads via Android settings, or remove ads entirely with Pro. You may lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

10. Sound rights (DMCA)

We claim no ownership of any sound clip, we neither sell nor re-license them, and no revenue comes from selling them (revenue is advertising only). Clips are gathered from public, widely circulated sources for entertainment. If you own the rights to a clip and object to its presence, email MohamadZalaf@outlook.com with the clip name or link and we will remove it within 72 hours of verification, free of charge. This notice is given in good faith and is not a waiver of anyone's rights.

11. Changes

Updates are published on this page with a new date; material changes are also noted in the app's release notes.

التطبيق مجاني ويُموَّل بالإعلانات. النافذة الإعلانية تُحمّل من شبكة Adsterra، وهي طرف ثالث مستقل قد يجمع تلقائياً: عنوان IP، نوع الجهاز ونظام التشغيل، لغة النظام، معرّف إعلاني/جهاز، ونوع الشبكة — وذلك لأجل عرض الإعلان وقياسه ومنع الاحتيال. لا نطّلع نحن على هذه البيانات ولا نخزّنها عندنا.

  • سياسة Adsterra: adsterra.com/privacy-policy
  • يمكنك تقليل التتبّع من إعدادات هاتفك: «إعلانات» → «حذف المعرّف الإعلاني» أو إعادة ضبطه.
  • إعلان المكافأة وفتح العرض يتمّان بضغطتك أنت، ولا تُفتح نوافذ إعلانية من تلقاء نفسها.

6. تحميل المؤثرات الصوتية

بعض المؤثرات تُنزَّل عند الطلب من خدمات استضافة صوتية عامة (مثل myinstants.com). عند التنزيل يظهر عنوان IP الخاص بك لخادم تلك الخدمة كأي تحميل عادي على الإنترنت، وتخضع حينها لسياسة خصوصية تلك الخدمة. بعد التنزيل يُخزَّن الملف في كاش التطبيق على جهازك.

7. تفعيل النسخة المدفوعة (Pro)

عند إدخال كود تفعيل، يُرسَل إلى سجل التحقق بصمة الكود (SHA-256) فقط — لا اسمك ولا بريدك ولا معرّف جهازك. البصمة لا تُقلَب إلى الكود الأصلي. يُخزَّن على الخادم نتيجة الطلب (الحالة: ساري/ملغى) وقد يُسجَّل عنوان IP في سجلات الخادم العادية لأجل الأمان ومنع إساءة الاستخدام. بلا إنترنت لا يتم التفعيل، ولا يُرسَل شيء.
