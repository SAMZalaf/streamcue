Sound Rights Notice — StreamCue Studio

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · package net.zalaf.streamcue

The app claims no ownership of any sound clip it contains. Clips are gathered from public, widely circulated sources on the internet (sound-sharing sites and communities). They are not produced by StreamCue Studio and do not come from any party sponsoring it.

Clips are used for entertainment and interactive content only — played in a stream, a video or a chat. They are not sold, not re-licensed, and not bundled into any commercial product, and no revenue is earned from selling them: the app's only revenue is advertising.

We respect the rights of authors and trademark owners and do not aim to harm any right. Many of these clips are widespread and used daily in user content, and many effectively act as promotion for their owner — but that does not replace your consent if you prefer not to appear.

Reporting and takedown (DMCA)

If you own the rights to a clip and believe its presence here is not permitted:

  • Email us at MohamadZalaf@outlook.com
  • Include the clip name or its link (a screenshot is enough) and a short statement of ownership.
  • We remove the clip within 72 hours of verification — without conditions, without charge, and without requiring any complex formal proof.
  • We will also replace it with a licensed clip, or credit it, if you prefer your name or work to stay in the app on a collaborative basis.

This notice is written in good faith for a fast, cooperative response. It waives no one's rights and is not an admission that any clip infringes a particular right — it is a practical commitment to respond quickly to any objection.

What we do to prevent the problem before it happens

  • Clip titles in the app are generic and descriptive — no game names or trademarks.
  • We use no logos, images or character names from any protected work.
  • We place no ads inside the clips and sell no sound "pack".
  • Any takedown request is actioned immediately, even if the request does not match the formal notice format.
